# Support resource pages: public content and interaction rules

These rules apply to housing/homelessness and Defence support-resource pages. The survey is out of scope.

- Present a finished public service. Never show demo, prototype, internal review or rollout labels in rendered public copy. Do not add a second page-identity block in the header. Keep the established Lutheran Care logo and brand treatment without claiming an approved official rollout.
- Main titles use the available content width. Do not force a character-based max-width, balanced wrapping, explicit line break or font reduction to make them fit. Check the actual desktop title width; allow ordinary wrapping on narrow screens.
- Start from concrete user tasks. Keep urgent accommodation, losing a home and finding a stable home distinct. Posting and time apart must lead to useful specific actions. Do not impose a tile quota or gather unrelated tasks under a broad label.

Keep tonight, losing a home and finding a stable home distinct, with tonight directly reachable. Preserve prominent Defence posting/time-apart entrances and the current typography, visible selected answers and automatic progression. Do not restore rejected introductions or mixed catch-all groups.
- Jacob’s rebuild correction (6 October 2026): show useful contacts immediately after a concrete task is chosen. Area, exact age, household composition and other relevant facts are optional narrowing beside results, never a compulsory eligibility interview. Unknown facts remain conditional enquiries.
