Respond when someone shows signs of distress

Ask for advice about the concern

ADF members and families can call the All-hours Support Line for mental-health advice and referral, including when seeking their own support.

ADF All-hours Support Line

NT-wide · Phone

Call 1800 628 036Visit service website
Who can use it
ADF members and families, including relatives seeking their own help.
Access and cost
Call directly, 24/7. Onward eligibility/fees vary. Immediate danger: 000.
Service details and sources

Mental-health advice, triage and referral to ADF or civilian support.

Ask about counselling or support for the family

Contact Open Arms to check the service and family rules and discuss its counselling or peer-support options.

Open Arms – Veterans & Families Counselling

NT-wide

Call 1800 011 046Visit service website
Who can use it
Eligible current/former ADF members and family; service-history rules apply.
Access and cost
Self-refer by phone, 24/7; professional referrals accepted. Free if eligible. Ask about eligibility and limited urgent help/referral.
Service details and sources

Military-aware counselling for individuals/couples/families, peer support and group programs.

Learn skills for future support

Check advertised Mental Health Protect courses and their participation rules. Training is not a substitute for help during a crisis.

Mental Health Protect – training

Online from NT

EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
Veteran-community adults 18+; excludes Defence/DVA staff attending for work and already-skilled health professionals.
Access and cost
Self-register for advertised free courses; limited places. Group approval/minimum numbers apply. Check time zone; no in-person NT course promised.
Service details and sources

Mental Health First Aid/LivingWorks training to recognise distress and connect people with help.
