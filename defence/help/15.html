Find support for a teenager or young adult

A young person can seek their own support, and family or friends can ask how to help.

Talk by phone or online

Check the age and hours on each service. Kids Helpline is for the young person; eheadspace also advises family, friends and carers supporting them.

Kids Helpline

NT-wide · Phone/web

Call 1800 551 800Visit service website
Who can use it
Young people aged 5–25; parents seeking their own support use Parentline.
Access and cost
Young person self-refers by phone/web; parent/carer may help connect. Free, 24/7; no referral needed.
Service details and sources

Private counselling about feelings, school, relationships and family life.
