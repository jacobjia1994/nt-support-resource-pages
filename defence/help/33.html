For anonymous military-aware counselling

Safe Zone lets callers choose how much identifying information to share, with legal and safety exceptions.

Safe Zone Support

NT-wide · Phone

Call 1800 142 072Visit service website
Who can use it
Current/former ADF personnel, veterans and families wanting anonymous military-aware support.
Access and cost
Self-refer by phone, 24/7. Free; no routine need to disclose identity or service history. Legal/safety exceptions apply.
Service details and sources

Anonymous counselling; callers choose how much identifying information to share.

For violence or sexual-misconduct advice

Use a safe device to contact 1800RESPECT. SeMPRO also offers anonymous advice to its listed Defence-connected groups without requiring a formal report.

1800RESPECT

NT-wide · Phone / online

Call 1800 737 732Text 04 5873 7732Visit service website
Who can use it
Anyone affected by domestic, family or sexual violence, including supporters.
Access and cost
Free counselling; phone, text, webchat and video available 24/7. Some mobile providers may charge. Use a safe device; immediate danger: 000.
Service details and sources

Counselling, safety planning and links to local services.

1800 737 732 Text 0458 737 732

1800 SeMPRO - sexual misconduct support

NT-wide · Phone / online

Call 1800 736 776EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
Current/ex-serving ADF, Defence APS/contractors, cadets and support networks; all genders.
Access and cost
Call/email directly, 24/7. Anonymous advice; no formal report required. No service fee advertised.
Service details and sources

Confidential sexual-misconduct support, including healthcare, legal and reporting options.

For ongoing eligible counselling

Ask Open Arms about eligibility and its support options, including any questions you have about confidentiality.

Open Arms – Veterans & Families Counselling

NT-wide

Call 1800 011 046Visit service website
Who can use it
Eligible current/former ADF members and family; service-history rules apply.
Access and cost
Self-refer by phone, 24/7; professional referrals accepted. Free if eligible. Ask about eligibility and limited urgent help/referral.
Service details and sources

Military-aware counselling for individuals/couples/families, peer support and group programs.
