Arrange travel for specialist treatment

Check the appropriate funding pathway and approval before booking travel.

For the NT patient travel scheme

Ask the treating healthcare provider about PATS and the office for your region. Eligibility, distance and referral rules apply; the subsidy may leave costs to pay.

PATS – Darwin/Top EndDarwin / Top End

Darwin / Top End

Call 08 8922 8135Visit service website
Who can use it
NT residents: Medicare, citizenship/six-month residency, specialist-referral and distance/repeated renal-oncology travel rules apply.
Access and cost
Healthcare provider applies; approval before booking. Interstate care needs NT public-specialist referral. Patient pays above subsidy.
Service details and sources

Specialist-care travel/accommodation subsidy; excludes costs covered/claimable through DVA, insurance or other government funding.

For a family at an eligible remote posting

Ask member administration about Defence specialist-treatment travel for an eligible accompanied resident family. Approval is needed before booking.

Defence family travel for specialist treatment in a remote location

Approved remote postings

Call 1800 333 362Visit service website
Who can use it
Eligible accompanied resident family at an approved remote posting; travel for a support person or dependant may be assessed.
Access and cost
Member applies before booking. Remote-location/treatment rules apply; incidental costs and extra baggage excluded.
Service details and sources

Approved travel for specialist medical/dental treatment unavailable locally.

For an eligible Veteran Card holder

Ask DVA about travel reimbursement or a booked car. Check attendant approval and the costs covered; PATS excludes costs claimable through DVA or other listed funding.

DVA Travel for Treatment and Booked Car with Driver

NT-wide · Treatment travel

Call 1800 550 455Visit service website
Who can use it
Eligible Veteran Card holders; booked-car service has age/medical rules. Approved attendants may travel.
Access and cost
Apply/book through MyService or phone. DVA-arranged transport is free; self-arranged costs may not be fully reimbursed.
Service details and sources

Travel reimbursement and eligible pre-arranged car transport.

1800 550 455 Arrange bookings in business hours
