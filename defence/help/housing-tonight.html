Sunrise Centre – homelessness program

Berrimah / Greater Darwin

Call 08 8997 1000EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
Single adults aged 18+; homelessness program, separate from alcohol/drug rehabilitation.
Access and cost
Self-refer: call, complete referral form and email centre. Assessment, vacancy and entry-dependent fees apply.
Service details and sources

Accommodation up to three months, intensive case management and longer-term housing/income support.

For a Defence family in a domestic crisis

Defence SAFE - short-term safe accommodation

NT-wide · Via DMFS

Call 1800 624 608Visit service website
Who can use it
Members or families who cannot remain home because of domestic crisis and have no alternative accommodation.
Access and cost
Contact family helpline 24/7 using a safe device. DMFS assesses placement, eligibility and costs; immediate danger: 000.
Service details and sources

Assessed temporary accommodation allowance through SAFE; family helpline can help with safety planning.

1800 624 608
