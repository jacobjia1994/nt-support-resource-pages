Support a young person who is an unpaid carer

Young carers can ask for practical support, time for themselves and information about study assistance.

Ask for carer support now

Contact Carer Gateway about counselling, coaching, practical help or respite. It supports unpaid caring for illness, disability, mental-health needs or frailty.

Carer Gateway

NT-wide

Call 1800 422 737Visit service website
Who can use it
Unpaid carers, including young carers, supporting disability, illness, mental-health needs or age-related frailty.
Access and cost
Free support. Call option 1, weekdays 8 am-5 pm local. Emergency respite enquiries 24/7; assessment and availability apply.
Service details and sources

Counselling, coaching, peer support and assessed practical help or respite.

Check study support and bursary announcements

Use Young Carers Network for support information. The 2026 bursary round has closed and 2027 dates are unconfirmed; check the next round’s rules when announced.

Young Carers Network – support and bursary

NT-wide · Phone/online

Call 1800 756 238Visit service website
Who can use it
Young unpaid carers; 2026 bursary generally ages 12–25 in eligible study; future rules unconfirmed.
Access and cost
Enquire directly; information free. 2026 applications closed; 2027 dates unconfirmed. Check new round’s eligibility before applying.
Service details and sources

Young-carer support information and bursary announcements; Carer Gateway for practical support now.

Make room for the young person’s own concerns

An eligible young person can contact Kids Helpline about their own feelings, school or relationships, without a referral.

Kids Helpline

NT-wide · Phone/web

Call 1800 551 800Visit service website
Who can use it
Young people aged 5–25; parents seeking their own support use Parentline.
Access and cost
Young person self-refers by phone/web; parent/carer may help connect. Free, 24/7; no referral needed.
Service details and sources

Private counselling about feelings, school, relationships and family life.
