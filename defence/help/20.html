Find mental-health care for yourself or family

Seek care for your own distress or ask how to support someone else.

Ask for a local or remote care pathway

Connect to Wellbeing can discuss where to start and the referral needed for a particular service. Intake is free; onward costs vary.

Connect to Wellbeing NT

NT-wide · Intake / referral

Call 1800 595 212Visit service website
Who can use it
NT residents needing a mental-health-care pathway.
Access and cost
Free intake; call for pathway advice. GPs, health professionals and community services can refer; telehealth psychiatry requires a GP referral. Onward costs vary.
Service details and sources

Intake, assessment and referral to local or remote care.

1800 595 212 Weekdays 8:30 am-5 pm NT

Check Defence-connected counselling

Ask Open Arms about your service history and family relationship if eligibility is uncertain. Non-full-time Reserve families can separately check the Reserve Assistance Program.

Open Arms – Veterans & Families Counselling

NT-wide

Call 1800 011 046Visit service website
Who can use it
Eligible current/former ADF members and family; service-history rules apply.
Access and cost
Self-refer by phone, 24/7; professional referrals accepted. Free if eligible. Ask about eligibility and limited urgent help/referral.
Service details and sources

Military-aware counselling for individuals/couples/families, peer support and group programs.

Reserve Assistance Program – counselling

NT-wide

Call 1300 687 327Visit service website
Who can use it
Non-full-time Reservists serving/committed this financial year; eligible resident family and same-household members.
Access and cost
Self-refer; family can attend independently. Defence funds up to four sessions/issue; up to four more may be approved. Defence APS EAP may reduce entitlement.
Service details and sources

Confidential counselling for personal, relationship, family and work concerns.

1300 687 327

For an adult wanting local walk-in support

Darwin and Katherine have free Medicare Mental Health Centres with no referral needed. Check the centre’s hours and directions before travelling.

Darwin Medicare Mental Health Centre

Darwin / Casuarina

Call 08 8914 6600Visit service website
Who can use it
Adults aged 18+; carers can seek guidance supporting someone in distress.
Access and cost
Free walk-in service; no referral or appointment. Call before travelling from outside Greater Darwin.
Service details and sources

Immediate support for distress, short-to-medium-term care and ongoing-care connections.

08 8914 6600 16 Scaturchio Street, Casuarina
