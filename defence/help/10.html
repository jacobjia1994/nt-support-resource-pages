When ordinary childcare does not fit

In Home Care Support Agency NT

NT-wide

Who can use it
Families meeting subsidy/In Home Care rules: unusual hours, isolation or complex needs.
Access and cost
Contact agency directly. Care fees/subsidy rules apply; local educators may be unavailable.

In a family care emergency

Emergency Support for Families Scheme

NT-wide · Via DMFS

Call 1800 624 608EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
A serving member’s resident family in an emergency when the member is away on duty or medically unable to provide care; assessment applies.
Access and cost
Call DMFS 24/7. Approved provider costs/reimbursements only; care arranged after assessment.
Service details and sources

Short-term approved childcare, family care, home help or respite.

1800 624 608

Paid care during a Defence move

Defence - childcare during a move

NT-wide · Defence-funded moves

Call 1800 333 362Visit service website
Who can use it
Eligible moving members with a child under 11 or with special needs, when no adult is available to care and paid care is used.
Access and cost
Include the expense in the relocation claim; keep evidence and confirm allowable costs. It does not provide a childcare place.
Service details and sources

Assistance with eligible childcare expenses during a Defence-funded move.

1800 333 362 Defence pay and conditions

If you are an unpaid carer
Carer GatewayNT-wide

NT-wide

Call 1800 422 737Visit service website
Who can use it
Unpaid carers, including young carers, supporting disability, illness, mental-health needs or age-related frailty.
Access and cost
Free support. Call option 1, weekdays 8 am-5 pm local. Emergency respite enquiries 24/7; assessment and availability apply.
Service details and sources

Counselling, coaching, peer support and assessed practical help or respite.

1800 422 737
