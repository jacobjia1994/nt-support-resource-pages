Plan leaving service and rebuilding routines

Start with transition planning, then check career and health support through the relevant programme.

Make a transition plan

Arrange an appointment with the NT Defence Transition Centre. Ask about family participation and approval for any funded training or advice.

NT Defence Transition Centre

NT-wide support · Robertson Barracks

Call 08 7971 6840EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
Permanent/Reserve members transitioning; program accessible for 24 months after separation.
Access and cost
Self-refer; arrange appointment/base access. Defence advice provided; specific funded training/advice requires approval.
Service details and sources

Transition planning for employment, skills and healthcare; family participation by arrangement.

08 7971 6840 transition.nt@defence.gov.au Building 0830, Robertson Barracks

Build a civilian career pathway

Ask Soldier On about careers, skills, education or employment support for the member or family.

Soldier On – career and education support

NT-wide phone/online · Darwin

Call 1300 620 380EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
Current/former Defence personnel and family members; adult mentoring requires age 18+.

Self-register or contact careers team. Core support free; external courses/tools may charge or have state-restricted funding.
Service details and sources

Career planning, skills/training pathways, employment resources, jobs hub and mentoring.

Check ongoing rehabilitation or mental-health cover

DVA rehabilitation and Non-Liability Health Care have distinct conditions. Check the member’s or veteran’s cover and the referral needed; family healthcare is separate.

DVA rehabilitation

NT-wide · DVA plan

Call 1800 838 372EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
Veterans with an accepted service-related condition, or some Veteran Payment recipients; assessment and rehabilitation-authority rules apply.
Access and cost
Self-request or referral with consent. Approved plan activities are DVA-funded.
Service details and sources

Skills for daily life, civilian healthcare navigation, work and social wellbeing; medical treatment and alcohol/drug rehabilitation are separate.

1800 838 372 rehabilitation@dva.gov.au

DVA Non-Liability Health Care - mental health

NT-wide · DVA / civilian providers

Call 1800 838 372EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
Full-time ADF veterans/members and some Reservists; service criteria apply. Family cover is separate.
Access and cost
Check DVA/MyService cover. Referral may be needed; treating provider must accept the relevant Veteran Card.
Service details and sources

Funded mental-health treatment without proving a service cause.
