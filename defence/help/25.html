For needs affected by a posting

Ask DMFS about formal special-needs recognition and the benefit being sought. The Special Needs Support Group can separately offer peer information and advocacy.

Defence support for families with special needs

NT-wide · Via DMFS

Call 1800 624 608EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
Permanent/continuous-full-time Reserve families with formally recognised special needs.
Access and cost
Contact DMFS for formal special-needs recognition and benefit assessment. Benefit limits apply; confirm other funding before claiming.
Service details and sources

Posting-related treatment/education continuity, housing, equipment, travel and practical moving help.

1800 624 608 specialneeds.families@defence.gov.au

For NDIS access or early-childhood support

Ask NDIA or the appropriate early-childhood partner how to start. A Defence connection alone does not establish eligibility or provide a funded plan.

NDIS access and early childhood support

NT-wide

Call 1800 800 110Call 1800 959 995Visit service website
Who can use it
NDIS residence/disability or early-intervention conditions; completed first application before 65. Defence affiliation alone insufficient.
Access and cost
Enquire directly; free. Greater Darwin early-childhood partner: ECA NT; elsewhere ask NDIA. Funded supports require eligibility/approved plan.
Service details and sources

NDIS access information and connections to early-childhood or coordination support.

NDIA 1800 800 110; ECA NT 1800 959 995

For service barriers, rights or a review

Contact the disability advocacy service covering your area. Family or carer involvement needs consent and must not create a conflict of interest.

DCLS Disability Rights Service

Top End

Call 08 8982 1111Visit service website
Who can use it
People with disability; family/carer involvement requires consent and no conflict of interest.
Access and cost
Contact directly; free, assessed advocacy. Ask about remote phone support, consent, capacity and appointments.
Service details and sources

Independent disability-rights advocacy, service access, complaints, NDIS reviews/appeals and education barriers.

08 8982 1111
