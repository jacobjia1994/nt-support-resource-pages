RSL – independent DVA claims advocacy

SA/NT phone · Darwin hub

Call 08 8100 7300EmailCall 08 7943 2800Visit service website
Who can use it
Veterans/current members and eligible dependants seeking DVA benefits; no RSL membership required.
Access and cost
Self-enquire by phone/form; underlying entitlement assessed. Darwin hub also hosts free RSL Queensland advocacy; book before visiting.
Service details and sources

Free claims preparation, entitlement guidance and review/appeal advocacy.

DVA - entitlements, treatment and claims

NT-wide phone · Darwin

Call 1800 838 372Visit service website
Who can use it
Members, veterans, Reservists and eligible family; each benefit has separate conditions.
Access and cost
No application/advice fee. Phone, MyService or enquiry form; phone weekdays 8 am-5 pm local.
Service details and sources

Benefit eligibility, claims, Veteran Cards and referral to the relevant DVA team.

1800 838 372 1 Briggs Street, Darwin Office weekdays 8:30 am-4:30 pm
