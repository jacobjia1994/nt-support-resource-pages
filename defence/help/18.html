Check changed family and care arrangements

Ask member administration how separation, shared care or a different household affects the particular Defence benefit. Recognition is assessed separately from counselling or legal help.

Defence family recognition and benefits

NT-wide · Member administration

Call 1800 333 362Visit service website
Who can use it
Serving members and family, including blended, separated, non-resident and extended-family situations; recognition is assessed.
Access and cost
Use current forms through member administration; ask about the actual relationship and care arrangements. Each benefit has separate rules.
Service details and sources

Clarify recognised family categories, benefit eligibility and changes after separation, shared care or family changes.
