Get support after a death by suicide

Support is available to family, friends and other people affected by the death.

Ask for practical and emotional support

Contact StandBy’s NT team about support by phone or a visit by arrangement, including connections to other local services.

StandBy Support After Suicide NT

NT-wide

Call 1300 727 247Visit service website
Who can use it
Anyone bereaved or affected by suicide, including families, friends, witnesses and communities.
Access and cost
Call NT team directly; free. Phone NT-wide, visits/travel by arrangement. Published daily 6 am–10 pm; confirm time zone.
Service details and sources

Practical/emotional support after suicide and connections to local support services.

For culturally led family or community support

Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people, families and communities can contact Thirrili about support after suicide or traumatic unexpected death.

Thirrili – culturally led postvention

NT-wide

Call 1800 805 801Visit service website
Who can use it
Aboriginal/Torres Strait Islander individuals, families/communities affected by suicide or traumatic unexpected death.
Access and cost
Call directly or community/service referral online. After-suicide contact 24/7. No fee published; discuss care plan and local arrangements.
Service details and sources

Culturally led postvention and coordinated family/community support after death.

If you need emotional crisis support now

Call Lifeline for immediate emotional support. For life-threatening danger or injury, call 000.

Lifeline

NT-wide · Phone

Call 13 11 14Visit service website
Who can use it
Anyone needing emotional crisis support, including people concerned about someone else.
Access and cost
Call directly, 24/7. Free crisis support. Immediate danger or life-threatening emergency: call 000.
Service details and sources

Immediate emotional support from a trained crisis supporter.
