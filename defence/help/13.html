NT-wide · Via Defence helpline

Call 1800 624 608Visit service website

Access and cost

Advice on school options, interstate curriculum differences and school transitions.

If enrolment, exclusion or learning support is a problem

Ask the teacher or principal about the government-school support pathway. You can also contact 54 reasons directly for independent student advocacy.

NT school wellbeing and inclusion (SWIPS)

NT government schools

Call 08 8944 9379Visit service website
Who can use it
NT government-school students and families; other schools use their own support pathways.
Access and cost
Speak to teacher/principal; only school can request SWIPS. General phone provides information. No separate fee stated.
Service details and sources

Support for wellbeing, behaviour, disability/additional needs and classroom adjustments.

08 8944 9379; contact the student’s school first
