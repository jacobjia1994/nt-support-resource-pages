All Defence help/Healthcare and caring

Arrange help at home during illness or injury

Emergency family care, service-related household help and Veteran Card home care have different conditions.

For a serving-family care emergency

Ask DMFS about ESFS when the member is away on duty or medically unable to care for their resident family. Approved short-term help is assessed.

Emergency Support for Families Scheme

NT-wide · Via DMFS

Call 1800 624 608EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
A serving member’s resident family in an emergency when the member is away on duty or medically unable to provide care; assessment applies.
Access and cost
Call DMFS 24/7. Approved provider costs/reimbursements only; care arranged after assessment.
Service details and sources

Short-term approved childcare, family care, home help or respite.

For needs caused by an accepted service condition

Ask DVA about an assessment for household services or attendant care. Approval depends on the condition and care need; it is not an automatic family-carer wage.

DVA Household Services and Attendant Care

NT-wide · DVA assessment

Call 1800 838 372EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
Veterans whose accepted service-related condition creates an assessed household or personal-care need.
Access and cost
Apply with assessment evidence. DVA considers reasonable available unpaid help; approved limits apply. No automatic family-carer wage.
Service details and sources

Approved household help and attendant care.

For low-level care at home with a Veteran Card

Ask VHC about assessment and provider allocation. Card, functional-need, contribution and duplicate-funding rules apply.

Veterans’ Home Care (VHC)

NT-wide

Call 1300 550 450Visit service website
Who can use it
Eligible Gold/White Card holders at home with functional limitations; British/Allied White Card holders: respite only. Limited bereaved-household continuation.
Access and cost
Call for assessment/provider allocation; residential respite needs aged-care assessment. No duplicate funded care. $5/hour: domestic $5/week, personal $10/week, garden $75/12-month caps; respite free within approved limits.
Service details and sources

Low-level domestic/personal care, safety-related home/garden maintenance and respite.
