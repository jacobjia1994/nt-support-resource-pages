Talk to a financial counsellor

Ask for help with bills, debts or creditor negotiations. Bravery Trust’s financial counselling has broader access than its separately assessed emergency aid.

National Debt Helpline

NT-wide · Phone/chat

Call 1800 007 007Visit service website
Who can use it
People in Australia experiencing financial difficulty; no Defence affiliation required.
Access and cost
Self-refer by phone/chat. Free advice/referrals, not loans or grants. Phone weekdays 9:30 am–4:30 pm; chat weekdays 9 am–8 pm. Confirm NT times.
Service details and sources

Confidential financial counselling about debts, bills, hardship and creditor negotiations.

DVA Acute Support PackageNT-wide · DVA assessment

NT-wide · DVA assessment

Call 1800 838 372Visit service website
Who can use it
Eligible under-65 veterans and related people; specified payment, crisis and family conditions apply.
Access and cost
DVA assessment; call to apply. Related people may apply independently from another household. Approved services generally paid directly; no ordinary bills, medical/dental care or emergency response.
Service details and sources

Short-term approved childcare, home help, tutoring, activities and counselling during crisis.
