All Defence help/Moving for a posting

Plan care or transport for a pet

A posting move, paid pet care and leaving an unsafe home need different arrangements.

For a Defence-funded move

Check the eligible pet transport and boarding costs before booking. You arrange and pay for the pet move, then claim eligible expenses with evidence.

Defence - relocating a pet

NT-wide · Defence-funded moves

Call 1800 333 362Visit service website
Who can use it
Members eligible for a Defence-funded move; pet and removal conditions apply.
Access and cost
Arrange and pay for the pet move; claim through the relocation process with evidence. Confirm limits before booking; reusable equipment/special cages excluded.
Service details and sources

Reimbursement of eligible reasonable pet transport and boarding expenses during relocation.

1800 333 362 Defence pay and conditions

For routine pet-care planning

Use RSPCA NT’s guidance and sitter-search links. Ask the chosen provider about fees and availability; this does not confirm free crisis boarding.

RSPCA NT – pet care information

NT-wide online / Berrimah

Call 08 8984 3795Visit service website
Who can use it
Pet owners; no Defence eligibility. Local sitter/boarding capacity is not confirmed.
Access and cost
Read guidance/enquire directly. Information free; sitters charge/set availability. No free crisis boarding confirmed; discuss safe-exit pets with violence services.
Service details and sources

Pet-care planning guidance and links to commercial sitter searches.

If a pet affects a safe exit

Tell a violence support service that a pet is part of your safety planning, using a safe device.

1800RESPECT

NT-wide · Phone / online

Call 1800 737 732Text 04 5873 7732Visit service website
Who can use it
Anyone affected by domestic, family or sexual violence, including supporters.
Access and cost
Free counselling; phone, text, webchat and video available 24/7. Some mobile providers may charge. Use a safe device; immediate danger: 000.
Service details and sources

Counselling, safety planning and links to local services.
