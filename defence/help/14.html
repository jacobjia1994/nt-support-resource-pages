Defence Education Liaison Officers (EDLOs)

NT-wide · Via Defence helpline

Call 1800 624 608Visit service website
Who can use it
Defence families moving schools or seeking education support.
Access and cost
Self-referral through the helpline; adviser appointment arranged. No advice fee stated.
Service details and sources

Advice on school options, interstate curriculum differences and school transitions.

Check the relevant education benefit

Defence posting assistance and DVA dependant education support have different eligibility and application rules. Confirm approval before spending where required.

Defence Education Assistance Scheme

NT-wide · Defence application

Call 1800 624 608EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
Children of eligible serving members affected by posting; benefit-specific age, study and family rules apply.
Access and cost
Apply through Defence; obtain approval before spending. Each benefit has limits.
Service details and sources

Assessed education, tutoring, boarding/tertiary and reunion-travel assistance.

1800 624 608 education.supportprograms@defence.gov.au

DVA Education Scheme (MRCAETS)

NT-wide · DVA application

Call 1800 838 372EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
Eligible veteran dependants; usually under 16 or aged 16-25 in full-time study/training. Disability/death conditions apply.
Access and cost
Claim through MyService/form or contact Education Schemes. Assistance depends on student circumstances.
Service details and sources

Education allowance, assessed tuition, counselling, travel and student support.

1800 838 372 educationschemes@dva.gov.au
