Emergency services

NT-wide

Call 000Visit service website
Who can use it
Anyone in immediate danger or with life-threatening illness or injury.
Access and cost
Call 000 immediately. Calls are free; give the exact location and immediate concern.
Service details and sources

Urgent ambulance, police or fire response.

For an urgent NT mental-health concern

Call the NT Mental Health Line for yourself or someone else to seek urgent advice and assessment.

NT Mental Health Line

NT-wide · Phone

Call 1800 682 288Visit service website
Who can use it
Anyone in NT concerned about their own or another person’s mental health.
Access and cost
Call directly for yourself or another person; no service fee. Available 24/7. Immediate danger: 000.
Service details and sources

Urgent mental-health advice, assessment and connection to NT public mental-health services.

For immediate emotional support or Defence-family advice

Lifeline provides emotional crisis support. ADF members and families can also call the All-hours Support Line for mental-health advice, triage and referral.

Lifeline

NT-wide · Phone

Call 13 11 14Visit service website
Who can use it
Anyone needing emotional crisis support, including people concerned about someone else.
Access and cost
Call directly, 24/7. Free crisis support. Immediate danger or life-threatening emergency: call 000.
Service details and sources

Immediate emotional support from a trained crisis supporter.

13 11 14
