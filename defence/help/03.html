Balance duty hours and family care

Changes to service hours and help with care have different application routes.

Discuss a change to service arrangements

Ask your supervisor about flexible work or service. Check the effects on pay, superannuation and housing as part of the application.

If ordinary childcare does not fit

Ask In Home Care about an assessment for unusual hours, isolation or complex needs. Eligibility and an available educator are separate questions.

Access and cost
Contact agency directly. Care fees/subsidy rules apply; local educators may be unavailable.

If a serving family has a care emergency

Ask DMFS about ESFS when the member is away on duty or medically unable to provide care. This is assessed emergency support for the member’s resident family.

Emergency Support for Families Scheme

NT-wide · Via DMFS

Call 1800 624 608EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
A serving member’s resident family in an emergency when the member is away on duty or medically unable to provide care; assessment applies.
Access and cost
Call DMFS 24/7. Approved provider costs/reimbursements only; care arranged after assessment.
Service details and sources

Short-term approved childcare, family care, home help or respite.
