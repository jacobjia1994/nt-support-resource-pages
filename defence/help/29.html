Soldier On – wellbeing and family connection

NT-wide telehealth · Darwin

Call 1300 620 380EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
Current/former Defence personnel and families; direct health/wellbeing work excludes under-12s; program conditions apply.
Access and cost
Self-register; psychology requires a GP/Medicare-professional referral and is via video in NT. Core services free; confirm local activities.
Service details and sources

Psychology/referral support, telehealth family groups and local connection activities.

Mates4Mates – Darwin wellbeing centre

Darwin / telehealth

Call 08 7943 2800EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
Current/former ADF members and spouse/partner/children; no deployment/minimum service; family can attend independently.
Access and cost
Self-register; GP DVA/Medicare referral for clinical appointments. Services free; check co-located provider billing. Telehealth by arrangement.
Service details and sources

Psychology, physiotherapy, exercise physiology, clinical groups, social connection and activities.

08 7943 2800 vfwc.darwin@mates4mates.org 39 Stuart Highway, Stuart Park; Ramirez Road entry
