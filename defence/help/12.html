Get help with parenting or children’s behaviour

Start with the concern you want help understanding: parenting stress, development or a child’s mental health.

Talk through the parenting concern

Parentline supports parents and carers with behaviour, family relationships and parenting stress. A young person seeking their own counselling can use the youth page below.

Parentline Queensland and NT

NT-wide · Phone / webchat

Call 1300 301 300Visit service website
Who can use it
Parents and carers of children in the Northern Territory or Queensland.
Access and cost
Self-refer by phone/webchat; counselling free, 1300 call charges may apply. Daily 5:30 am–11:30 pm NT time (6 am–midnight AEST).
Service details and sources

Counselling about parenting stress, children’s behaviour and family relationships.
