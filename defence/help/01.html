Toll Transitions – Defence relocation

NT-wide

Call 1800 819 167EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
Defence members/families with approved eligible relocation; changed circumstances need supporting documentation.
Access and cost
Member applies online; contact Toll if no notice within 21 days of posting order. Costs follow entitlements; extras may be excluded.
Service details and sources

Coordinates approved removals, inventories, move plans and relocation problems.

1800 819 167; defencecare@tollgroup.com
