Find or keep suitable housing

Nowhere safe to stay tonight?

Use the branch that fits: a serving-family housing arrangement, a tenancy problem or nowhere safe to stay.

For serving-family housing or two homes

Ask DHA about suitable housing or Rent Allowance, and member administration about living-apart arrangements. Confirm the relevant approval before signing a lease or committing to travel.

If you have nowhere safe to stay

For a domestic crisis, ask the family helpline about assessed SAFE accommodation. For other homelessness support, Central Intake accepts online referrals during its telephone outage and responds within 48 business hours; it is not help for tonight. Compare the local accommodation programmes below by area and household. Immediate danger: call 000.

Defence SAFE - short-term safe accommodation

NT-wide · Via DMFS

Call 1800 624 608Visit service website
Who can use it
Members or families who cannot remain home because of domestic crisis and have no alternative accommodation.
Access and cost
Contact family helpline 24/7 using a safe device. DMFS assesses placement, eligibility and costs; immediate danger: 000.
Service details and sources

Assessed temporary accommodation allowance through SAFE; family helpline can help with safety planning.
