Get help with alcohol, drugs or gambling

You can ask for help about your own use or the effect on a family member.

For alcohol or drug concerns

Ask Amity about counselling, or use the alcohol and drug phone or online options. Family and friends can also seek support.

Amity alcohol drug and gambling counselling

NT-wide telehealth · Darwin

Call 08 8944 6565Visit service website
Who can use it
People aged 14+ affected by alcohol, drugs or gambling, and affected family/friends.
Access and cost
Free. Self-referral by phone or online; third-party referral needs consent. Darwin appointments or telehealth.
Service details and sources

Specialist counselling for substance use, gambling and related concerns.

For debts or financial pressure

Contact the National Debt Helpline about bills, hardship or creditor negotiations. It provides advice rather than loans or grants.

National Debt Helpline

NT-wide · Phone/chat

Call 1800 007 007Visit service website
Who can use it
People in Australia experiencing financial difficulty; no Defence affiliation required.
Access and cost
Self-refer by phone/chat. Free advice/referrals, not loans or grants. Phone weekdays 9:30 am–4:30 pm; chat weekdays 9 am–8 pm. Confirm NT times.
Service details and sources

Confidential financial counselling about debts, bills, hardship and creditor negotiations.
