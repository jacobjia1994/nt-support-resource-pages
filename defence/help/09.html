Ask Childcare Connect about care that fits your location and routine, including school-hours or vacation care. Search support does not guarantee a place.

Check Child Care Subsidy

Check the subsidy rules and complete the claim and enrolment process. Ask the provider what you would pay after subsidy and hourly caps.

Services Australia - Child Care Subsidy

NT-wide · Centrelink

Call 13 61 50Visit service website
Who can use it
Eligible families paying for approved care; generally children 13 or younger and not at secondary school. Residence and immunisation rules apply.
Access and cost
Claim through Centrelink/myGov and confirm enrolment. Fee gaps and hourly caps apply; subsidised hours do not guarantee a place. Claims can backdate only within limits.
Service details and sources

Income-tested help with fees. Eligible families receive at least 72 subsidised hours per fortnight; some qualify for 100.

136 150 Families line, weekdays 8 am-8 pm

Enquire about the Palmerston or Tindal centre

Contact the relevant One Tree service about a place, the current fee and Defence priority. Tindal attendance also needs base access.

One Tree Kids Brigade – childcare

Palmerston

Call 08 8931 3036EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
Young children; Defence-family priority applies; community places depend on availability.
Access and cost
Enquire/enrol directly; priority/place assessed. User pays: published $124.56–$134.41/day before subsidy; confirm current age-based quote.
Service details and sources

Long-day nursery, toddler and preschool care at the Palmerston centre.
