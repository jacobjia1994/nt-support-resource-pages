Get support after a member or veteran dies

Grief support, dependant benefits and practical family help have different pathways.

Choose grief support

Open Arms can discuss counselling eligibility. Griefline’s listed service provides grief resources, peer access and enquiry support rather than immediate counselling.

Open Arms – Veterans & Families Counselling

NT-wide

Call 1800 011 046Visit service website
Who can use it
Eligible bereaved family; parents/siblings have narrower, specified bereavement pathways. Adult children may qualify through service-history rules.
Access and cost
Self-refer by phone, 24/7; professional referrals accepted. Free if eligible. Ask about eligibility and limited urgent help/referral.
Service details and sources

Military-aware counselling for individuals/couples/families, peer support and group programs.

Griefline and SANE grief support access

NT-wide · Phone / online

Call 1300 845 745Visit service website
Who can use it
Adults aged 18+ with grief/loss, and adult supporters.
Access and cost
Free enquiry support; call costs may apply. Identifiable caller number required. Not immediate counselling or crisis response.
Service details and sources

Grief information, SANE Recovery Club registration, resources and peer forums.

1300 845 745 Weekdays 10 am–8 pm AEST/AEDT Excludes public holidays

Ask about dependant or funeral benefits

Contact DVA about the death, service and dependency conditions for the particular benefit. Counselling is a separate enquiry.

DVA - support after a member or veteran dies

NT-wide · DVA claims

Call 1800 838 372Visit service website
Who can use it
Partners, eligible dependent young people and other dependants; service/death and dependency conditions apply.
Access and cost
Contact DVA/MyService. No claim fee; Legacy/RSL may assist with claims. Bereavement counselling is a separate service.
Service details and sources

Assessed dependant compensation, funeral assistance and continuing benefits.

1800 838 372

Ask for practical family support

Legacy assesses support for families affected by loss of life or health through service. The Veteran and Family Wellbeing Agency can help connect other services.

Northern Territory Legacy

Darwin / regional NT

Call 04 5726 7200Visit service website
Who can use it
Partners/children of veterans who lost life or health through service; individual assessment applies.
Access and cost
Self-refer; call before visiting or request regional Legatee. No support charge advertised; financial help individually assessed.
Service details and sources

Social contact, family support, assessed relief, claims assistance and children’s education/activity help.

0457 267 200 11 Nemarluk Drive, Ludmilla

Veteran and Family Wellbeing Agency

NT-wide · Phone/online

Call 1800 823 922Visit service website
Who can use it
Veterans/families, including serving families preparing for transition; referred services have separate conditions.
Access and cost
Self-refer by helpline/form; others may refer. No navigation charge published. Weekdays 8:30 am–5 pm caller’s local time.
Service details and sources

Finds wellbeing supports, connects services and coordinates more complex needs.

DVA Acute Support PackageNT-wide · DVA assessment

NT-wide · DVA assessment

Call 1800 838 372Visit service website
Who can use it
Eligible bereaved partners or parenting grandparents after a service-related death; dependency, time and crisis rules apply.
Access and cost
DVA assessment; call to apply. Related people may apply independently from another household. Approved services generally paid directly; no ordinary bills, medical/dental care or emergency response.
Service details and sources

Short-term approved childcare, home help, tutoring, activities and counselling during crisis.

1800 838 372
