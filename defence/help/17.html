Get help with relationship strain

Choose relationship counselling or ask a military-aware service about your situation.

Ask about individual, couple or family counselling

Relationships Australia NT accepts direct enquiries. Both people need to speak with intake before joint counselling; ask about the income-based fee.

Relationships Australia NT – counselling

NT-wide · Offices/phone/video

Call 1300 458 600Visit service website
Who can use it
Individuals, couples and families.
Access and cost
Self-refer; no referral required. Income-based fees; ADF members do not automatically receive NT Government/PFES employee concessions. NT phone/video available. Both people must speak with intake before joint counselling.
Service details and sources

Relationship counselling for stress, parenting, grief and separation.

1300 458 600 Darwin, Katherine, Alice Springs offices

Check military-aware counselling options

Open Arms and the Reserve Assistance Program have different service and family rules. If your eligibility is unclear, ask the service rather than ruling yourself out.

Open Arms – Veterans & Families Counselling

NT-wide

Call 1800 011 046Visit service website
Who can use it
Eligible current/former ADF members and family; service-history rules apply.
Access and cost
Self-refer by phone, 24/7; professional referrals accepted. Free if eligible. Ask about eligibility and limited urgent help/referral.
Service details and sources

Military-aware counselling for individuals/couples/families, peer support and group programs.

Reserve Assistance Program – counselling

NT-wide

Call 1300 687 327Visit service website
Who can use it
Non-full-time Reservists serving/committed this financial year; eligible resident family and same-household members.
Access and cost
Self-refer; family can attend independently. Defence funds up to four sessions/issue; up to four more may be approved. Defence APS EAP may reduce entitlement.
Service details and sources

Confidential counselling for personal, relationship, family and work concerns.
