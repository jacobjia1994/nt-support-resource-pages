Get support as a carer and take part in care decisions

Ask for help with unpaid caring, or independent advice about the person’s care and rights.

For your own caring role

Ask Carer Gateway about counselling, coaching, respite or assessed practical support. This is for unpaid caring for illness, disability, mental-health needs or frailty.

Carer Gateway

NT-wide

Call 1800 422 737Visit service website
Who can use it
Unpaid carers, including young carers, supporting disability, illness, mental-health needs or age-related frailty.
Access and cost
Free support. Call option 1, weekdays 8 am-5 pm local. Emergency respite enquiries 24/7; assessment and availability apply.
Service details and sources

Counselling, coaching, peer support and assessed practical help or respite.

For disability or aged-care rights

Contact the appropriate advocacy service about decisions or service problems. Ask how the person’s consent and your role as a supporter are handled.

DCLS Disability Rights Service

Top End

Call 08 8982 1111Visit service website
Who can use it
People with disability; family/carer involvement requires consent and no conflict of interest.
Access and cost
Contact directly; free, assessed advocacy. Ask about remote phone support, consent, capacity and appointments.
Service details and sources

Independent disability-rights advocacy, service access, complaints, NDIS reviews/appeals and education barriers.

08 8982 1111

Disability Advocacy Service (DAS)

Central Australia / Barkly

Call 08 8953 1422EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
People with disability; family/carer involvement requires consent and no conflict of interest.
Access and cost
Contact directly; free, assessed advocacy. Covers Alice Springs, Amoonguna, Tennant Creek and surrounding Central Australia; confirm appointments.
Service details and sources

Independent disability-rights advocacy, service access, complaints, NDIS reviews/appeals and education barriers.

Older Persons Advocacy Network (OPAN)

NT-wide · Phone/online

Call 1800 700 600Visit service website
Who can use it
Older people using/seeking government-funded aged care, and people supporting them.
Access and cost
Self-refer by phone or online enquiry; free. National line connects callers to their state/territory advocacy organisation.
Service details and sources

Independent advocacy about aged-care rights, decisions and problems with services.

For several connected veteran-family needs

Ask the Veteran and Family Wellbeing Agency to help navigate services and coordinate more complex support. Each referred programme has its own conditions.

Veteran and Family Wellbeing Agency

NT-wide · Phone/online

Call 1800 823 922Visit service website
Who can use it
Veterans/families, including serving families preparing for transition; referred services have separate conditions.
Access and cost
Self-refer by helpline/form; others may refer. No navigation charge published. Weekdays 8:30 am–5 pm caller’s local time.
Service details and sources

Finds wellbeing supports, connects services and coordinates more complex needs.

DVA Acute Support PackageNT-wide · DVA assessment

NT-wide · DVA assessment

Call 1800 838 372Visit service website
Who can use it
Eligible under-65 veterans and related people; specified payment, crisis and family conditions apply.
Access and cost
DVA assessment; call to apply. Related people may apply independently from another household. Approved services generally paid directly; no ordinary bills, medical/dental care or emergency response.
Service details and sources

Short-term approved childcare, home help, tutoring, activities and counselling during crisis.
