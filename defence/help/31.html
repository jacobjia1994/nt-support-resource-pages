Talk with a peer supporter

QLife supports people with questions about sexuality, gender, identity or relationships, and the people supporting them. Check its hours; it is not emergency clinical care.

QLife

NT-wide · Phone / webchat

Call 1800 184 527Visit service website
Who can use it
LGBTIQA+ people, people with questions, and people supporting them in Australia.
Access and cost
Self-refer by phone/webchat; free. Daily 3–9 pm local; last calls 8:40 pm. Overseas/hidden VPN addresses may block chat.
Service details and sources

Peer support/referrals about sexuality, gender, identity and relationships; not emergency clinical care.

Ask about military-aware counselling

Contact Open Arms about your service history and family relationship if eligibility is unclear.

Open Arms – Veterans & Families Counselling

NT-wide

Call 1800 011 046Visit service website
Who can use it
Eligible current/former ADF members and family, including adult children; qualifying service and relationship rules apply.
Access and cost
Self-refer by phone, 24/7; professional referrals accepted. Free if eligible. Ask about eligibility and limited urgent help/referral.
Service details and sources

Military-aware counselling for individuals/couples/families, peer support and group programs.

Clarify recognition for a particular benefit

Ask member administration about your actual relationship and care arrangements. Recognition and each funded benefit are assessed separately.

Defence family recognition and benefits

NT-wide · Member administration

Call 1800 333 362Visit service website
Who can use it
Serving members and family, including blended, separated, non-resident and extended-family situations; recognition is assessed.
Access and cost
Use current forms through member administration; ask about the actual relationship and care arrangements. Each benefit has separate rules.
Service details and sources

Clarify recognised family categories, benefit eligibility and changes after separation, shared care or family changes.
