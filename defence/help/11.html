Get support during pregnancy or early parenthood

Ask for practical nurse guidance, support with your mental health or local child-health care.

Ask a nurse about pregnancy or parenting

Pregnancy Birth and Baby can discuss feeding, development and early parenting questions by phone or video.

Pregnancy Birth and Baby

NT-wide · Phone/video

Call 1800 882 436Visit service website
Who can use it
Expecting parents, parents of children up to five, and friends/relatives in Australia.
Access and cost
Self-refer by phone/video. Free nurse support; mobile charges may apply. Daily NT time: 6:30 am–11:30 pm during AEST; 5:30 am–10:30 pm during AEDT.
Service details and sources

Nurse guidance about pregnancy, feeding, development and parenting.

1800 882 436

Talk about your mental health as a parent

Contact PANDA for pregnancy or early-parenthood counselling and peer support. Check its hours; use urgent help for a crisis.

PANDA National Helpline

NT-wide · Phone

Call 1300 726 306Visit service website
Who can use it
Expecting/new parents and supporters; published resources cover children up to 12 months.
Access and cost
Call directly; free support, 1300 charges may apply. Not emergency response. Weekdays 9 am–7:30 pm; Saturday/Sunday 9 am–4 pm AEST/AEDT.
Service details and sources

Counselling and peer support for mental health during pregnancy and early parenthood.
