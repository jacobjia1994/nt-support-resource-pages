For current Defence-family questions

Ask the family helpline about the issue you are facing. Advice is available to ADF members, Reservists and families; funded benefits have separate rules.

Defence Member and Family Helpline

NT-wide · Phone / email

Call 1800 624 608EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
ADF members, Reservists and families; funded benefits have separate rules.
Access and cost
Free advice; contact directly, 24/7. Email for non-urgent enquiries.
Service details and sources

Practical advice and referrals for posting, absence, children, family stress and transition.

1800 624 608 memberandfamilyhelpline@defence.gov.au

For counselling or wellbeing care

Open Arms and Mates4Mates have different service and family eligibility rules. Ask about your circumstances and the particular clinical or social programme.

Open Arms – Veterans & Families Counselling

NT-wide

Call 1800 011 046Visit service website
Who can use it
Some Commonwealth/allied veterans with eligible White Card/reciprocal pathways; foreign service alone is insufficient.
Access and cost
Self-refer by phone, 24/7; professional referrals accepted. Free if eligible. Ask about eligibility and limited urgent help/referral.
Service details and sources

Military-aware counselling for individuals/couples/families, peer support and group programs.

Mates4Mates – Darwin wellbeing centre

Darwin / telehealth

Call 08 7943 2800EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
Current/former ADF members and spouse/partner/children; no deployment/minimum service; family can attend independently.
Access and cost
Self-register; GP DVA/Medicare referral for clinical appointments. Services free; check co-located provider billing. Telehealth by arrangement.
Service details and sources

Psychology, physiotherapy, exercise physiology, clinical groups, social connection and activities.

08 7943 2800 vfwc.darwin@mates4mates.org 39 Stuart Highway, Stuart Park; Ramirez Road entry

For navigating veteran-family services

Ask the Veteran and Family Wellbeing Agency to help connect services, including when a serving family is preparing for transition.

Veteran and Family Wellbeing Agency

NT-wide · Phone/online

Call 1800 823 922Visit service website
Who can use it
Veterans/families, including serving families preparing for transition; referred services have separate conditions.
Access and cost
Self-refer by helpline/form; others may refer. No navigation charge published. Weekdays 8:30 am–5 pm caller’s local time.
Service details and sources

Finds wellbeing supports, connects services and coordinates more complex needs.

1800 823 922
