Open Arms and the Reserve Assistance Program have different service and family conditions. Contact them about your actual circumstances if the rules are unclear.

Open Arms – Veterans & Families Counselling

NT-wide

Call 1800 011 046Visit service website
Who can use it
Eligible current/former ADF members and family; service-history rules apply.
Access and cost
Self-refer by phone, 24/7; professional referrals accepted. Free if eligible. Ask about eligibility and limited urgent help/referral.
Service details and sources

Military-aware counselling for individuals/couples/families, peer support and group programs.

Reserve Assistance Program – counselling

NT-wide

Call 1300 687 327Visit service website
Who can use it
Non-full-time Reservists serving/committed this financial year; eligible resident family and same-household members.
Access and cost
Self-refer; family can attend independently. Defence funds up to four sessions/issue; up to four more may be approved. Defence APS EAP may reduce entitlement.
Service details and sources

Confidential counselling for personal, relationship, family and work concerns.

1300 687 327

Ask about scheduled support calls

TeamTALK supports NT residents with mild distress or emerging risk. It does not provide acute care; use the urgent-help page if the situation has become a crisis.

TeamTALK

NT-wide · Phone

Call 1800 832 600EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
NT residents with mild mental illness/distress or emerging risk; excludes acute-care needs.
Access and cost
Self-refer by phone/email; other referrals accepted. Free. Weekdays 9 am–8 pm; weekends noon–8 pm NT.
Service details and sources

Low-intensity distress/isolation support, scheduled calls and links to local mental-health services.

1800 832 600; teamtalk@teamhealth.asn.au
