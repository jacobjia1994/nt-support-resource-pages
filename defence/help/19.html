Talk through safety and support options

1800RESPECT supports anyone affected by violence. SeMPRO provides confidential sexual-misconduct support for its listed Defence-connected groups, including anonymous advice without a formal report.

1800RESPECT

NT-wide · Phone / online

Call 1800 737 732Text 04 5873 7732Visit service website
Who can use it
Anyone affected by domestic, family or sexual violence, including supporters.
Access and cost
Free counselling; phone, text, webchat and video available 24/7. Some mobile providers may charge. Use a safe device; immediate danger: 000.
Service details and sources

Counselling, safety planning and links to local services.

1800 737 732 Text 0458 737 732

Ask about safe accommodation or legal options

For a domestic crisis with no alternative accommodation, ask the family helpline about assessed Defence SAFE support. Legal Aid NT can explain legal options; regional shelters and specialist services are listed below.

Defence SAFE - short-term safe accommodation

NT-wide · Via DMFS

Call 1800 624 608Visit service website
Who can use it
Members or families who cannot remain home because of domestic crisis and have no alternative accommodation.
Access and cost
Contact family helpline 24/7 using a safe device. DMFS assesses placement, eligibility and costs; immediate danger: 000.
Service details and sources

Assessed temporary accommodation allowance through SAFE; family helpline can help with safety planning.

1800 624 608

DVA Acute Support PackageNT-wide · DVA assessment

NT-wide · DVA assessment

Call 1800 838 372Visit service website
Who can use it
Eligible under-65 veterans and related people; specified payment, crisis and family conditions apply.
Access and cost
DVA assessment; call to apply. Related people may apply independently from another household. Approved services generally paid directly; no ordinary bills, medical/dental care or emergency response.
Service details and sources

Short-term approved childcare, home help, tutoring, activities and counselling during crisis.
