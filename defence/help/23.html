All Defence help/Healthcare and caring

Arrange healthcare and continue treatment

Civilian-family healthcare and serving-member healthcare use different systems.

For general or civilian-family health advice

Call healthdirect for nurse advice and help finding appropriate healthcare. The advice line does not require a Defence connection.

healthdirect

NT-wide · Phone / online

Call 1800 022 222Visit service website
Who can use it
Anyone in Australia; no Defence connection required.
Access and cost
Free advice, 24/7. Onward treatment may charge fees. Life-threatening emergency: 000.
Service details and sources

Registered-nurse advice and help finding appropriate healthcare.

1800 022 222

For a serving member away from base or after hours

Use 1800 IM SICK for serving-member nurse advice and triage. Routine garrison care uses the member’s assigned centre; those centres are listed below.

1800 IM SICK - serving-member nurse triage

NT-wide · Phone

Call 1800 467 425Visit service website
Who can use it
Current ADF personnel in Australia; not civilian-family healthcare.
Access and cost
Call directly, 24/7. No helpline fee advertised. Life-threatening emergency: 000.
Service details and sources

After-hours nurse advice and triage, or help when away from an on-base health facility.

1800 467 425

Check dependant cover or posting-related continuity

The Family Health Program requires registered eligible dependants. Posting-related special-needs support has separate formal recognition and benefit assessment.

ADF Family Health Program

NT-wide · Claims online

Call 1300 561 454EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
Registered dependants of permanent members or Reservists on continuous full-time service.
Access and cost
Register first; claim after Medicare/insurance. Registered dependants may continue up to 12 months after the member dies while serving.
Service details and sources

Eligible GP gaps plus pooled $800 per registered dependant/year for other eligible care.

1300 561 454 Adf.familyhealth@navyhealth.com.au

Defence support for families with special needs

NT-wide · Via DMFS

Call 1800 624 608EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
Permanent/continuous-full-time Reserve families with formally recognised special needs.
Access and cost
Contact DMFS for formal special-needs recognition and benefit assessment. Benefit limits apply; confirm other funding before claiming.
Service details and sources

Posting-related treatment/education continuity, housing, equipment, travel and practical moving help.
