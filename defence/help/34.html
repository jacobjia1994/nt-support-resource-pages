For practical Defence-family questions

The family helpline can discuss posting, absence, children or family stress and connect you with relevant support.

Defence Member and Family Helpline

NT-wide · Phone / email

Call 1800 624 608EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
ADF members, Reservists and families; funded benefits have separate rules.
Access and cost
Free advice; contact directly, 24/7. Email for non-urgent enquiries.
Service details and sources

Practical advice and referrals for posting, absence, children, family stress and transition.

For a mental-health-care pathway

Ask Connect to Wellbeing about local or remote care, referrals and the next step for your concern.

Connect to Wellbeing NT

NT-wide · Intake / referral

Call 1800 595 212Visit service website
Who can use it
NT residents needing a mental-health-care pathway.
Access and cost
Free intake; call for pathway advice. GPs, health professionals and community services can refer; telehealth psychiatry requires a GP referral. Onward costs vary.
Service details and sources

Intake, assessment and referral to local or remote care.

1800 595 212 Weekdays 8:30 am-5 pm NT

For several connected veteran-family needs

The Veteran and Family Wellbeing Agency can help find services and coordinate more complex needs, including for serving families preparing for transition.

Veteran and Family Wellbeing Agency

NT-wide · Phone/online

Call 1800 823 922Visit service website
Who can use it
Veterans/families, including serving families preparing for transition; referred services have separate conditions.
Access and cost
Self-refer by helpline/form; others may refer. No navigation charge published. Weekdays 8:30 am–5 pm caller’s local time.
Service details and sources

Finds wellbeing supports, connects services and coordinates more complex needs.

1800 823 922
