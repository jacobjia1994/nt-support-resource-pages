For eligible partner career expenses

Apply for PEAP and obtain approval before starting the eligible career service. Reimbursement limits apply.

Partner Employment Assistance Program (PEAP)

NT-wide · Online applications

Call 1800 624 608EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
Recognised resident partner living in Australia, not on continuous full-time service; member on continuous full-time service, posted in Australia.
Access and cost
Partner applies; approval before service, reimbursement within three months. Pay above limit; approved BUSY coaching has no upfront cost. May live separately.
Service details and sources

Approved career coaching, licensing/checks/registration and vocational training, up to $1,500/financial year.

1800 624 608 partner.employment@defence.gov.au

Carer GatewayNT-wide

NT-wide

Call 1800 422 737Visit service website
Who can use it
Unpaid carers, including young carers, supporting disability, illness, mental-health needs or age-related frailty.
Access and cost
Free support. Call option 1, weekdays 8 am-5 pm local. Emergency respite enquiries 24/7; assessment and availability apply.
Service details and sources

Assessed tailored support packages may include tutoring, educational supplies, training courses or study equipment to help unpaid carers continue education alongside caring.
