Ask about support or respite for an unpaid carer

Carer Gateway can discuss your caring role and assess practical help or respite. Emergency respite enquiries are available at any time.

Carer Gateway

NT-wide

Call 1800 422 737Visit service website
Who can use it
Unpaid carers, including young carers, supporting disability, illness, mental-health needs or age-related frailty.
Access and cost
Free support. Call option 1, weekdays 8 am-5 pm local. Emergency respite enquiries 24/7; assessment and availability apply.
Service details and sources

Counselling, coaching, peer support and assessed practical help or respite.

Ask about aged care or dementia support

My Aged Care can explain assessment and care options. For dementia or memory concerns, the National Dementia Helpline also supports families and carers.

My Aged Care

NT-wide

Call 1800 200 422Visit service website
Who can use it
Care needs: age 65+; First Nations or homeless/at-risk people 50+. Family help requires consent.
Access and cost
Call or use website; Services Australia can assist. Contact/assessment information free; care contributions, eligibility and availability vary.
Service details and sources

Aged-care assessment and help finding home support, residential care or respite.

National Dementia Helpline

NT-wide · Phone / chat / email

Call 1800 100 500EmailVisit service website
Who can use it
People with dementia, memory concerns or mild cognitive impairment, and families/carers.
Access and cost
Self-refer by phone, chat or email; request personalised support online. Free, available 24/7.
Service details and sources

Dementia information, emotional support and connections to community services.

1800 100 500; helpline@dementia.org.au

For Veteran Card home care or aged-care rights

Ask VHC about assessed home support for an eligible card holder. OPAN gives independent advice about aged-care rights and service problems.

Veterans’ Home Care (VHC)

NT-wide

Call 1300 550 450Visit service website
Who can use it
Eligible Gold/White Card holders at home with functional limitations; British/Allied White Card holders: respite only. Limited bereaved-household continuation.
Access and cost
Call for assessment/provider allocation; residential respite needs aged-care assessment. No duplicate funded care. $5/hour: domestic $5/week, personal $10/week, garden $75/12-month caps; respite free within approved limits.
Service details and sources

Low-level domestic/personal care, safety-related home/garden maintenance and respite.

1300 550 450

Older Persons Advocacy Network (OPAN)

NT-wide · Phone/online

Call 1800 700 600Visit service website
Who can use it
Older people using/seeking government-funded aged care, and people supporting them.
Access and cost
Self-refer by phone or online enquiry; free. National line connects callers to their state/territory advocacy organisation.
Service details and sources

Independent advocacy about aged-care rights, decisions and problems with services.
