Arrange a language interpreter

Use TIS National or the Aboriginal Interpreter Service for the language needed. Ask the destination service to arrange or fund interpreting and confirm any charges.

TIS National – interpreting

NT-wide · Phone

Call 13 14 50Visit service website
Who can use it
People in Australia with limited or no English needing service access.
Access and cost
Call with language, organisation and number. Available 24/7; destination must accept/open. Local call charge; confirm payer before accepting interpreting charges.
Service details and sources

Connects a language interpreter to the organisation the caller needs.

Make a relay call

The National Relay Service supports d/Deaf, hard-of-hearing and speech-impaired callers. Register, choose the communication channel and provide the service’s number.

National Relay Service

NT-wide · Relay channels

Visit service website
Who can use it
People who are d/Deaf, hard of hearing or have speech communication difficulties.
Access and cost
Register through Access Hub; select channel and provide destination number. Channel hours vary; ordinary phone/data costs may apply.
Service details and sources

Text, captions, Auslan or assisted-voice relay calls to other services.
